Use your location for zones?

  • Used only to tell whether you're inside a zone, such as the Main Hall.
  • Other people never see where you are, and positions never appear in messages.
  • Coordinates aren't stored. Your last reading lasts up to 5 minutes, and zone membership lapses after 10.
  • GPS is approximate. You can leave a zone or turn location off at any time, which deletes what we hold.
